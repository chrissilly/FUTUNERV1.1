## [MAC] NRC error-handling audit

**Scope:** Companion audit to the cross-ref doc. Where the cross-ref
audited *protocol* correctness (right bytes?), this one audits *error
handling* (wire-observable failure modes handled correctly?). Reuses
the cross-ref agent's PDF text extracts (had to re-extract a 3-PDF
subset — cleanup deleted them at end of prior turn). Builds on the
cross-ref doc + MM NRC grep + orchestrator code-path inventory.

**What landed:**

- `hw_reference/NRC_ERROR_HANDLING_AUDIT.md` (~38 KB) — full §A.1
  NRC catalogue, MM-observed NRC inventory (12 distinct SID/NRC
  pairs in 67 frames), per-NRC audit rows (6 columns: NRC / ISO
  meaning / VW80126 per-phase guidance / MM observed line refs /
  our handling / recommendation), findings sorted by priority,
  recommendations + tracking against P-04/P-07/P-08.

**Headline finding — 🔴 critical pre-HIL-3 bug:**

**Pending-loop gap.** Four orchestrator call sites use bare
`uds_exchange` (single send + single recv) followed by
`uds_assert_positive`. If the ECU's first response is `7F xx 78`
(requestCorrectlyReceived-ResponsePending), `uds_assert_positive`
sees `rx[0] == 0x7F` and returns ESP_FAIL — the orchestrator bails
before the ECU sends its actual final response.

Affected:
1. `preflight_ecureset_and_resync` line 271 (preflight ECUReset, ×2)
2. `phase_fingerprint` line 476 (fingerprint write)
3. `phase_section_request_download` line 522 (RequestDownload, ×5)
4. `phase_ecu_reset` line 640 (final closeout ECUReset)

**Sites 1 & 4 are CONFIRMED-FAILING.** MM observed `7F 11 78` 6
times in the dev-RS7 capture — twice for each of the 3 ECUReset
events. On the next real-bench Phase 3 dispatch our orchestrator
will reach the preflight ECUReset, receive `7F 11 78` from the MG1,
fail the assert, and abort with "reset failed" while the ECU was
just waiting to send `51 01`.

Sites 2 & 3 are vulnerable but didn't fire in MM's dev-RS7 capture
(spec allows pending; different ECU variants might emit it).

**Why this wasn't caught earlier:** the shadow harness sends positive
responses synchronously without `7F xx 78` pending. The bug
reproduces only on a real ECU. Bug 2 fix (2026-05-17) added
`surface_nrc_or_continue` to the SA path but didn't audit the
pending-loop coverage across the rest of the orchestrator.

**Second 🔴 finding (less severe but real):** post-SA phase NRCs
(8 phases — fingerprint, erase, RequestDownload, TransferData,
TransferExit, CheckMemory, CheckProgDeps, final reset) don't fire
the `MDG1_FLASH_PHASE_NRC_RECEIVED` progress event before bailing.
Operator sees "td failed" instead of `7F 36 70 UDNA` or `7F 31 22
CNC`. Same Bug 2 fix pattern needs to extend to these 8 sites.

**Key takeaways:**

1. **The orchestrator is byte-correct on every critical UDS step.** The
   shadow diff harness contract (P-08) of "wire-exact match vs MM
   after masking SA/fingerprint/TP" is achievable; this doc audits each

2. **SA2 VM is byte-exact to spec** — proven against the 5 reference
   vectors in SA2-060331-V10 Tabelle 4 / §2.5. The interpreter is
   variant-agnostic; the per-variant script comes from the variant
   manifest.
3. **The 3-cycle preflight pattern** (preconditions → 10 02 → 11 01 ×3
   with the third skipping the reset) is **Bosch MG1 bootloader

   CommunicationControl) and **MM-style post-reset cleanups** — both
   spec-aligned-but-NRC'd-anyway. Defer, watch for related NRCs on
   non-RS7 variants during P-07.
